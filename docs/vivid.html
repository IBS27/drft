Same room, more color in it.

Four directions for the web app. Each keeps the Stillness palette and today's layouts, and changes the type, how much the vermilion does, and how surfaces feel.

Why today reads as faint

  • Tiny spaced capitals. Most labels are 10 to 11px uppercase with 0.3em tracking. Hard to scan, easy to miss.
  • Light weight everywhere. Helvetica at 300 on off-white thins every word.
  • Gray on gray. Rows and links sit in mid grays until hovered, so nothing feels clickable.
  • The accent is one 6px dot. The vermilion is the brand and it barely shows up.
EmberReadable type, and the vermilion becomes a real accent. CardsThoughts become objects you can pick up. PosterBig confident type and blocks of solid color. GlowNew thoughts run warm and cool as they age.

Today

The current web app, rebuilt here so the four directions have something to be compared against.

What works, what doesn't

Keep: the calm layout, the single field, the warm grays, the vermilion as "now".

Change: almost every label is a 10px spaced capital in mid gray, the body is set at weight 300, and nothing has a surface. The page is quiet to the point of being hard to read, and nothing about it is memorable at a glance.

Ember

The smallest step. Readable type, real contrast, and the vermilion grows from a single dot into the accent that marks "now", "back today" and "keep".

  • Geist at 400, sentence case
  • Rows in ink, not gray
  • Solid vermilion Keep
  • Tinted "Back today" strip
  • Soft 10px surfaces

Ember at a glance

Type
Geist, 400 body, 500 to 600 headings, sentence case, 13px minimum
Accent
Vermilion fills Keep, marks the open row, tints "Back today" and counts
Surfaces
Flat page, soft warm tints for anything that is "now". Notes sit on ruled lines with a vermilion margin, no box.
Radius
10px surfaces, full pill for buttons and chips

Tradeoff: the safest change and the easiest to ship, but it fixes readability more than it makes drft memorable. It still looks like a calm notes app.

Cards

Every thought gets a surface. They sit on a slightly deeper canvas, lift when you touch them, and capture becomes a card you're about to add to the pile.

  • Satoshi at 400 to 700
  • Raised cards, warm shadows
  • Vermilion Keep button
  • Returned thought gets a red edge
  • Sentence-case labels

Cards at a glance

Type
Satoshi, 400 body, 500 thoughts, 700 labels. Nothing under 13px.
Accent
Vermilion on the Keep button, the returned thought's edge, and notes.
Surfaces
Canvas one step deeper than today; cards raised with warm-tinted shadows.
Radius
Surfaces 16px, mini cards 12px, every control a full pill.

Tradeoff: the most physical and friendly of the four, but more boxes on screen. The collection reads busier than today, and the rail shows fewer thoughts per screen because each one is a card.

Poster

Your thoughts set like headlines. Big confident type, sharp edges, thick rules, and the returned thought as a solid block of vermilion.

  • Cabinet Grotesk at 700 to 800
  • Big bold group words, no micro caps
  • Returned thought as a vermilion block
  • 2px ink rules, square corners
  • Solid ink buttons

Poster at a glance

Type
Cabinet Grotesk 700 to 800 for thoughts and headings, Satoshi 500 for everything small.
Accent
Vermilion only as a solid block: the returned thought and its tag.
Surfaces
Flat. Structure comes from 2px ink rules and solid ink fills.
Radius
0 everywhere.

Tradeoff: the most memorable of the four and the easiest to read, but it trades the quiet, private feeling for confidence. A long thought set at 48px takes over the screen.

Glow

Now is warm. The vermilion turns into light: it glows behind what you're writing, and thoughts cool as they get older.

  • General Sans, nothing under 13px
  • A halo behind capture
  • Rows warm by age
  • Frosted rail
  • Warmth shows how close a link is

Glow at a glance

Type
General Sans 400 and 500. Sentence-case labels, nothing under 13px.
Accent
Vermilion as light: halos, warm washes, and a heat scale by age.
Surfaces
A frosted rail, warm tinted cards, a lit center.
Radius
Pills for anything you press, 20px for surfaces.

Tradeoff: the most atmospheric of the four and the most to tune. Push the tints a little too far and the rail turns pink; keep them too faint and the heat scale stops reading.

Pick one, or mix: the type from one and the surfaces from another. Light and dark toggle is at the top right.
